Rules & Policies

1. Certificate Authenticity

Certificates generated through CertificateHub must contain truthful and accurate information. Users must not create certificates intended to falsely represent qualifications, achievements or education.

2. No Fraudulent Certificates

CertificateHub must not be used to create, alter or distribute fraudulent certificates or documents intended to deceive another person, organization or institution.

3. Verification

Each certificate may be associated with a unique certificate number and verification record. Users should use the verification system whenever authenticity needs to be confirmed.

4. Account Responsibility

Users are responsible for keeping their account credentials secure. Do not share your password with other people.

5. Personal Information

Only provide information that you are authorized to use. Do not upload another person's private information without appropriate permission.

6. Responsible Use

CertificateHub is designed to help organizations, educators, trainers, businesses and individuals create and manage legitimate certificates.

7. Prohibited Activities

Users must not use CertificateHub for fraud, impersonation, deception, unlawful activities, harassment or any activity that violates applicable laws.

8. Service Availability

CertificateHub may be updated, improved or temporarily unavailable from time to time as features and security systems are maintained.

9. Certificate Ownership

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights and permission to issue certificates using names, logos, signatures and other organization information.

10. Agreement

By using CertificateHub, you agree to use the service responsibly and comply with these rules and policies.
